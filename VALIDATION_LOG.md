## Gate question

Will enough ordinary Victorian outdoor customers pay A$39.95–49.95 for a compact, illustrated, decision-first emergency field guide—and can it be made with a contribution margin that repays professional development?

## Pre-declared Gate 2 criteria

The project should not enter full production unless the combined evidence meets these conditions or a clearly stronger equivalent:

1. At least 150 qualified email sign-ups at blended cost no greater than A$6 each.
2. At least 20 refundable A$5 reservations or 10 full-price preorders after customers see the proposed price.
3. At least three retailer/partner expressions of interest that include likely quantity, wholesale discount and acceptable RRP.
4. Timed prototype tests show untrained readers can reach and correctly interpret critical actions without coaching.
5. A$39.95 or higher does not collapse purchase intent.
6. Print and fulfilment quotes indicate at least A$12 contribution through an Amazon/retail-compatible channel or A$18 through direct sale.
7. Medical, botanical and Indigenous collaboration pathways are credible, scoped and affordable.
8. A quoted base-case three-year model recovers the full production budget without assuming future regional editions.

These thresholds are management hypotheses, not market norms. They must be set before traffic is purchased so disappointing results cannot be rationalised after the event.
