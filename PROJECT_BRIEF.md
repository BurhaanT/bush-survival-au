# Project brief

## Purpose

Research, create, review and maintain an exceptionally useful personal field guide for someone who is lost, injured or stranded in the Victorian bush.

The guide is being created for the founder's personal use. Commercial publication, market validation, KDP economics and regional series development are parked indefinitely. The existing commercial research is retained as background only.

## Current book definition

The book is:

> A decision-first, illustrated Victorian bush emergency and survival field guide: what to do in the first minutes, first night and until rescue, with carefully verified Victorian natural-resource knowledge for longer emergencies.

The guide is activity-agnostic. It assumes the reader is in Victorian bush for some reason—walking, camping, driving, fishing, working or otherwise—and now needs to survive and possibly obtain help. Chapters are organised by the problem and required decision, not by the activity that preceded it.

Markdown chapter files are the editable source of truth. A combined Markdown edition and the PDF are generated outputs. The geographic scope is broad Victoria only.

## Product principles

- Teach priorities and decisions before techniques.
- Design for an untrained, stressed reader in a real emergency.
- Put preservation of human life before ordinary activity rules when a person faces an immediate risk of death or really serious injury and an otherwise prohibited act is the only reasonable response. Keep this narrow, proportionate and fact-dependent; it is not permission for convenience, routine food gathering or planned non-compliance.
- Follow the [plain-language writing rules](EDITORIAL_STYLE_GUIDE.md): familiar words, short clear sentences, explained technical terms, ordered actions and reader checks. Preserve every essential safety condition when simplifying.
- Organise guidance by situation rather than hiking, camping, 4WD, fishing or another activity; mention an activity only when it changes the safest action.
- Be specific to Victorian environments, services, laws and seasons.
- Prefer a compact, carried product over encyclopaedic coverage.
- Use visuals only when they improve action or identification.
- Keep food below rescue, temperature, injury and water priorities.
- Use authoritative sources and preserve traceability.
- Apply the [universal content validation gate](CONTENT_VALIDATION_GATE.md) to every factual claim, instruction, image, map and recommendation.
- Treat medical, botanical, water, fire, movement/navigation and food claims as high consequence.
- For every practical medical/first-aid entry, present two reviewed paths: action with the relevant supplies from a comprehensive first-aid kit, and action when those supplies are unavailable.
- Include Aboriginal cultural and ecological knowledge only through consent, collaboration, attribution, governance and benefit sharing appropriate to Country.
- Never use AI-generated botanical imagery as identification evidence.
- Keep uncertain or incompletely reviewed material out of the usable PDF.
- Preserve editable, modular source files so one section can be corrected and the complete PDF regenerated.

## Project gates

1. Commercial market assessment — **completed and parked**.
2. Personal-edition architecture and evidence standard — established; updated as research finds gaps.
3. Chapter research and evidence packets — in progress; see CURRENT_STATE for dated coverage and unresolved topics.
4. Drafting and visual development — readable working prose now fills all 15 canonical chapters, with preceding warnings and evidence-confidence labels. Some specialist procedures and natural-resource entries remain incomplete; no factual diagrams or field-ready prose approved.
5. Cross-chapter safety, consistency and usability review — authoring checks in progress; independent specialist and reader tests outstanding.
6. Combined Markdown edition — regenerate the readable working manuscript after edits; approved field-ready edition still gated.
7. PDF generation, page rendering and visual QA — last production stage.

## Success test

The guide must be trustworthy, usable under stress, specific to Victorian conditions, easy to update and materially safer than relying on an uncited collection of survival tips. Commercial return is not a success criterion.

## Founder role

The founder supplies direction, personal preferences and approvals. Research, source management, cross-checking, drafting, comparison and routine project administration should be carried by the project workflow and appropriate human specialists.
