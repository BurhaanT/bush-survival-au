## Medical, movement, water, fire and aquatic gate — working edition 0.12

This wave improves readable decision support, replaces an over-engineered stove-water transfer sequence with the official rolling-boil/cool/use core, adds a bounded current-CFA caught-in-the-open sequence and retains a low-burden conditional fire pathway. No dependable zero-equipment water treatment is claimed, and no exact product route is approved. This does not create specialist approval. The large-type diagrams and exact wording must still be reviewed and tested before field use.

## Review levels

- **Level A — critical:** foreseeable death/serious injury if wrong. Independent qualified review, edition sign-off and update trigger required.
- **Level B — significant:** injury, poisoning, illegality or rescue delay possible. Specialist review and two-source minimum.
- **Level C — operational:** lower-consequence usability or factual issue. Editorial/field verification.
