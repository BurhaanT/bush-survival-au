### Recognise it

- Signs a person without medical training can recognise
- Uncertainty and conditions that can look similar
- Warning signs that need urgent help

### When to call for help or move to medical care

- When to call 000 or use another way to get help
- When moving the person could cause harm
- What information to give

### Do this immediately

- Shared actions, whether or not you have a kit
- Dangers to the person, you and others nearby
- Priorities before the two paths split

### With a first-aid kit

- Item IDs from `FIRST_AID_KIT_REGISTER.md`
- Exact evidence-supported use
- What the reader must already have practised or been trained to do
- Limits and stop conditions

### Without a first-aid kit

Use this section too if the kit is missing the listed items.

- Safest evidence-supported action with no specialist supply
- Ordinary material only if specifically validated for this use
- What cannot be replaced safely
- Whether missing equipment changes what to check or how urgently the person needs rescue or medical care

### Do not do this

- Common harmful responses
- Unsafe improvised substitutes
- Outdated or overseas advice that does not apply

### Keep checking for

- Changes and check interval only where evidence supports one
- Signs the person is getting worse and what help is needed

### While waiting or moving to care

- How to position and protect the person, stay in contact and keep notes
- Limits on moving the person and what to tell the people taking over care

### Evidence and verification

- Three or more independent checks
- Deliberate contradiction search
- Applicable Australian/Victorian scope
- Clinical review outcome
- Post-layout recheck
