# Comprehensive first-aid kit register

**Status:** Framework only. No item or medical use has been approved.  
**Purpose:** Define what “with a comprehensive first-aid kit” means in the book and keep every kit-assisted instruction consistent with the equipment actually specified.

The kit is defined by capabilities and verified uses, not by a commercial product label. Inclusion of an item does not imply that an untrained reader is competent to use it.

## Inclusion gate

For each proposed item:

- identify the exact evidence-supported task it enables;
- establish whether a simpler or lighter item provides the same capability;
- define size, quantity or performance only from current evidence;
- record training prerequisites and actions outside a lay reader's competence;
- record shelf life, temperature/moisture limits and inspection/replacement triggers;
- cross-check every medical page that names it;
- provide a no-kit path in the medical entry without pretending that an unsafe substitute is equivalent;
- obtain clinical review of both the kit-assisted and no-kit wording.

## No-kit rule

“Without a kit” assumes no specialist medical supply. Ordinary clothing or materials may be used only when the exact use passes the same Level 3 evidence and clinical-review gate. Where no safe replacement exists, the book must say so and explain the effect on monitoring, rescue or evacuation urgency.
