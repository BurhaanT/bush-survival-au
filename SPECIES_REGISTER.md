# Species register

**Status:** Eight conditional plant-food entries, one public-land rejection/private-site boundary, twenty education-only, hazard-recognition or cultural non-harvest pictured plant entries, four unapproved fish-recognition candidates, two unapproved rabbit/hare reference entries, and further explicit rejection or withheld entries drafted. No page is approved as a first-time field-identification route, and no conditional consumption reminder is field-ready.
**Purpose:** Prevent a candidate name from becoming published advice before identity, distribution, season, preparation, toxicity and cultural authority are independently established.

## Inclusion rule

A species should be considered only if it is common enough in at least one relevant Victorian environment, offers meaningful emergency utility, can be distinguished reliably by a non-expert using the proposed format, and earns its page space relative to rescue, shelter, water and first aid.

No consequential identification or consumption claim may rely on one source. Level 3 entries require three independent checks under [CONTENT_VALIDATION_GATE.md](CONTENT_VALIDATION_GATE.md). Plant taxonomy and Victorian distribution should be anchored in VicFlora/Royal Botanic Gardens Victoria; animal entries require the equivalent Victorian or Australian taxonomic and fisheries authorities. Food/toxicity/preparation claims require separate authoritative evidence and qualified review. Aboriginal names and uses require an authorised collaboration pathway; publication in an old text is not sufficient permission.

## Status vocabulary

- **Candidate:** mentioned for possible research only.
- **Researching:** sources being assembled.
- **Working entry:** source-supported prose and explicitly limited pictures may be read under D-031–D-034; independent review and full diagnostic coverage are incomplete. Not approval to identify or consume a specimen.
- **Evidence complete:** two-source minimum and source conflicts recorded.
- **Expert reviewed:** identified reviewer has signed off on the specified edition content.
- **Cultural review complete:** authorised scope, attribution and permissions recorded where relevant.
- **Approved / rejected:** editorial decision recorded with reason.

## Required record for every eventual species

- Accepted scientific name and synonyms at edition date
- Common names and risk of ambiguous common-name use
- Traditional/local names, Country, source, authority and permitted context
- Overall growth form and size
- Leaves/phyllodes, arrangement, venation and texture
- Bark/stem
- Flowers and flowering season
- Fruit/seed and season
- Habitat and Victorian bioregional distribution
- Usable part and exact emergency relevance
- Preparation and dose/quantity limits, if safe and appropriate
- Toxic parts and life stage
- Dangerous lookalikes and a “do not use unless” rule
- Image set and diagnostic annotations
- Three independent checks for consequential claims, including an authoritative Victorian botanical source and separate edibility/toxicity confirmation
- Source contradiction notes
- Botanist/toxicologist reviewer, version and date
- Identification confidence and editorial decision
