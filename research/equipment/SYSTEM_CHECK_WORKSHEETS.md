# Equipment checks: what is ready and what is still needed

**Prepared:** 7 September 2026  
**Status:** Unexecuted worksheets. No purchase, account activation, hire, outside contact or physical test is authorised.  
**Purpose:** Turn the equipment research into small, traceable decisions without buying an entire kit.

## Read this first

The guide is about surviving and getting help, not buying these brands. Product names identify examples whose instructions can be checked. General guidance should explain the required job, limits and missing-equipment option. Brand-specific sequences belong only in clearly labelled panels for that exact verified model.

This file summarises existing research, not fresh product verification. Prices and availability remain dated snapshots in the linked assessments. A listing does not prove local stock, the supplied variant, remaining battery life, current instructions or fitness for use.

Do not buy every comparator. First resolve the desk questions, ask whether suitable inert trainers or demonstrators can be borrowed under an agreed arrangement, and request a priced test plan for one candidate. A demonstrator is not evidence that a later purchased unit is identical. Verify the supplied unit separately.

## WS-01 — Personal locator beacon

**Research file:** [PLB candidates](PLB_CANDIDATE_ASSESSMENT.md). **Equipment:** EQ-003. **Stage:** desk shortlist; sample/independent review needed.

- First candidate: Australian-coded GME MT620GR / `MT620GRAUS`. MT610G / `MT610GAUS` is the lower-cost comparator; Australian-supplied Ocean Signal PLB1 is the smaller alternative.
- Before requesting a sample or price: ask for country coding, exact supplied label/manual, manufacture and battery-expiry dates, included retention/flotation items, Australian service route and written service cost. Do not publish a beacon's full identifier in a public research file.
- Proposed harmless task: use an inert trainer to find, retrieve and interpret the controls and indication examples; explain what acknowledgement does and does not mean. Check access after hypothetical pack/vehicle separation.
- Critical errors: mistaken distress activation during practice; inability to reach the device; interpreting receipt as a promised rescue time; an attachment arrangement that introduces an injury hazard.
- Boundary: any powered self-test requires exact manual limits and separate approval. Never run live SOS to see whether rescuers respond.

## WS-02 — Phone, power bank and cables

**Research file:** [phone/power system](PHONE_POWER_SYSTEM_ASSESSMENT.md). **Equipment:** EQ-001–EQ-002. **Stage:** cannot finalise exact system without the actual handset and intended carried devices.

- Conditional comparison: Nitecore NB10000 Gen4 and BioLite Charge 40 PD. Do not buy either before the ports, charging requirements and sample instructions match the actual devices.
- Missing record: handset model/software, working primary and spare cables, dry storage, normal use pattern, relevant accessibility needs and existing equipment. These become user-specific only at the test stage; the generic book does not need the founder's private device details now.
- Proposed ordinary-use task: an authorised tester records charging and normal emergency-information tasks with the exact system, then repeats after the proposed storage interval. Measure what happens; do not infer a number of charges from the label.
- Critical errors: wet-port charging, damaged equipment used, shared loss of both cables, forgotten charging, unreadable status or a phone-power claim that implies network coverage.
- Boundary: no battery abuse, intentional overheating, freezer exposure or destructive water testing.

## WS-04 — Shelter, ground insulation and dry storage

**Research file:** [shelter system](SHELTER_AND_INSULATION_SYSTEM_ASSESSMENT.md). **Equipment:** EQ-008–EQ-012 and EQ-026. **Stage:** fit and whole-system comparison required.

- Existing shortlist includes Blizzard BPS02, Z Lite SOL Regular, Ether Light XR Insulated Regular, conventional dry insulation and a separately assessed shade option. They perform different jobs and are not interchangeable.
- Before a sample plan: identify intended occupants and fit range, clothing, ground layer, dry insulation, storage dimensions, carry arrangement and repair/replacement rules. Choose a complete system to compare, not all products.
- Proposed harmless task: supervised dry deployment in benign conditions, fit/access observation, harmless packing and closure checks. Use an unoccupied setup for any authorised simulated rain test.
- Critical errors: missing ground/insulation layer; blocked access or breathing; inability to open or escape; loss of critical gear when a pack is removed; a cold enclosure repurposed as unsafe hot-weather shade.
- Boundary: no deliberate hypothermia, heat illness, smoke exposure, storm camping, flood/avalanche simulation or sleeping outside a reviewed system's limits. A successful indoor demonstration provides no temperature rating.

## WS-05 — Map, compass and non-fire signals

**Research file:** [navigation and signalling](NAVIGATION_AND_SIGNALLING_EQUIPMENT_ASSESSMENT.md). **Equipment:** EQ-005–EQ-007, EQ-019–EQ-022 and EQ-025. **Stage:** trip-area and exact-user package not yet defined.

- Research candidates: current trip-area Vicmap paper/offline package; Suunto M-3 G versus A-30 SH; Fox 40 Classic; SOL Rescue Flash; VS-17 CIV versus Axis/Milde V sheet; Fenix HM23 V2.0 and a separately stored Petzl e+LITE.
- Resolve missing panel dimensions/materials, actual map area/scale, compass variant, exact light batteries and current manuals before a purchase list is issued.
- Proposed task: tabletop map/coordinate readback and offline access, supervised familiar-site orientation, and controlled contrast/retention comparisons. Use inert signal demonstrations until the supervisor approves hearing, light and reflection arrangements.
- Critical errors: wrong map or coordinate format; unexpected app/network dependency; compass interference missed; signal practice mistaken for real distress; signalling prompts unsafe movement.
- Boundary: no live aircraft, road traffic, vessels or unsuspecting people as test targets; no close high-output whistle tests. Navigation equipment never independently authorises a walkout.

## WS-06 — Natural-water treatment

**Research file:** [treatment candidates](WATER_TREATMENT_SYSTEM_ASSESSMENT.md). **Equipment:** EQ-016–EQ-017. **Stage:** do not issue a purchase/test sequence while the chemical product and treatment evidence remain unresolved.

- Conditional candidates: QuickDraw `13903` plus an exact unresolved chemical product and separate clean vessel; Wayfarer `WPA0101` as the alternative under assessment. Do not infer an Aquatabs dose from another tablet strength or use-case.
- Required document enquiry: exact intended tablet formulation, market authorisation, supplied label, treatment conditions and supporting performance evidence. Seek the missing full filter evidence identified in the assessment.
- Proposed harmless workflow test: only after approval, use known drinkable tap water and a reviewer-approved visible tracer to reveal dirty/clean handling mistakes. Mark test liquid and vessels clearly; do not drink test liquid.
- Critical errors: dirty cap/outlet crosses a clean opening; wrong tablet or dose; timing/temperature condition missed; source hazard wrongly assumed removable; backup shares the same failure.
- Boundary: no drinking untreated or deliberately contaminated water, pathogen challenges, improvised dosing or chemical experimentation. A tracer test checks handling, not pathogen removal.

## WS-07 — Carried water and an optional boiling system

**Research file:** [carriage and boiling](POTABLE_WATER_CARRIAGE_AND_BOILING_ASSESSMENT.md). **Equipment:** EQ-013–EQ-015 and EQ-018. **Stage:** cold-container checks can be planned; heat system remains document/reviewer blocked.

- Candidate containers: two separately closed 1 L clean hard bottles, a scalable Watercell X 4 L clean reserve, a separate dirty collection bag, and supplemental vehicle cans where relevant. These are capacity comparisons, not a universal carry quantity.
- Resolve actual fit, reach, closure differences and full/part-full load; verify the proposed Pro Quip 11 L `1010` supply before any vehicle-container order.
- Proposed harmless task: authorised leak, label/readback, packing, separation and clean tap-water transfer checks at room temperature. Keep test water out of electronics and medical supplies.
- For any future heat task, use WS-08 first. The PocketRocket 2, fuel, stand and final vessel are one system; nominal pot volume does not establish usable output or stability.
- Critical errors: vehicle water counted as on-person water; dirty/clean role-switching; fire-control water double-counted; a hot fill into a container not approved for it.
- Boundary: no hot-water or flame test until current exact documents, site and supervision are approved. No universal water quantity is established here.

## WS-08 — Ignition and fire control

**Research file:** [ignition and fire control](IGNITION_AND_FIRE_CONTROL_ASSESSMENT.md). **Equipment:** EQ-018. **Stage:** exact candidates identified; no lighting or firefighting procedure approved.

- Take the assessment's lighter/match pair to the reviewer as candidates. Obtain the current Australian stove manual and labels before explaining any sequence.
- Ask for an appropriate site/control plan, not simply a shopping recommendation for an extinguisher. Keep drinking water and fire-control provision separate.
- Initial tasks use only approved inert props and fault cards. Any later flame task requires separate written approval and qualified supervision.
- Critical errors: using advice for another stove; modified safety features; fire equipment assumed to make unsafe conditions acceptable; stove moved into enclosed shelter; a kitchen blanket treated as personal bushfire protection.
- Boundary: no Total Fire Ban flame testing, deliberate leaks, heated canisters, uncontrolled flames or exposure to an actual fire threat.

## Recommended order and spending control

1. **Now, without purchases:** complete the document register and prepare bounded specialist/referral questions. Keep unresolved entries out of usable prose.
2. **After approval to enquire:** obtain reviewer availability/scope/fees and missing manufacturer documents. Do not book a review or disclose the full private project without further approval.
3. **After accepting a limited review scope:** ask that reviewer to prioritise the few demonstrations needed to settle the emergency core. Use permitted trainers or existing suitable equipment before considering purchases.
4. **Before each purchase or test:** present the exact item, total cost, purpose, limits, return terms and responsible tester for approval. Do not convert all eight sheets into one large shopping list.
5. **After actual checks:** record failures and changes, repeat affected tests, and review the exact words and visuals. Only then consider a usable edition.
