# RB-003 — Envenomation and toxicology review

**Required reviewer:** Australian clinical toxicologist or clinician with current Australian envenomation expertise.  
**Review object:** IM-189, the text-led working snakebite decision card/pressure-immobilisation concept map permitted under D-050, plus the snake/PIT and Victorian dangerous-bite/sting pages in the dated review prototype. IM-189 is not approved technique art.  
**Scope boundary:** allergy-device/product decisions go to RB-004; aquatic rescue mechanics go to an accredited aquatic specialist.

## Content IDs in scope

- `SNAKE-001` to `SNAKE-005`;
- `BITE-001`, `BITE-002`;
- `SPIDER-001` to `SPIDER-003`;
- `MARINE-003` to `MARINE-005` for toxicology/PIT scope;
- the no-cut, no-suction, no-wash, no-capture, movement and CPR intersections;
- `KIT-007`, `KIT-008`, relevant use of `KIT-009`, and any proposed exact bandage/splint;
- D-050/IM-189 boundaries: text-led core actions and intended coverage only; exact equipment, pressure/checks, unknown-site, upper-limb, self-application, no-kit and lone/no-contact mechanics withheld.

## Decisions requested

1. Confirm the Victorian suspected-snakebite recognition/escalation rule without species-identification dependence.
2. Decide whether IM-189 may retain the local-first two-bandage wording as one ANZCOR-permitted method without implying that it is proven best; separately approve or reject any future exact wrap geometry, pressure intent/check, clothing treatment, limb order, splint role, completion check and failure warning.
3. Resolve the invisible/unknown-site branch without encouraging a mark search or silently switching methods, and decide the exact upper-limb and child-sizing boundaries.
4. Decide whether self-application can be taught and whether any broad-clothing/material fallback or improvised splint can be illustrated safely; all current mechanics are blocked.
5. Decide the lone/no-contact movement rule; current default is immobility and help coming to the person, and IM-189 intentionally supplies no walkout rule.
6. Resolve mouse-spider inclusion and the uncertainty threshold for a suspected Victorian funnel-web/mouse-spider bite without importing Sydney epidemiology unchanged.
7. Confirm explicit non-PIT branches for redback/other spiders, ticks, insects, jellyfish and fish/stingray injury.
8. Confirm PIT/monitoring for blue-ringed octopus, cone shell and sea snake, including the priority of ventilation/CPR.
9. Review the proposed prohibition on live capture and ensure optional photography cannot delay care or falsely clear risk.
10. Decide what exact kit bandage/splint specification can be recommended and how heat, moisture, ageing and training affect readiness.

## Required output

- decision label and exact wording for every in-scope content ID;
- an explicit keep/revise/withdraw decision for every IM-189 panel and withheld branch, without treating the working concept map as proof of technique;
- approved treatment-selection matrix or corrections;
- explicit rejected/omitted techniques;
- exact equipment specification and trainer test if PIT is retained;
- referral of marine heat/vinegar or allergy matters outside toxicology scope as appropriate;
- post-layout review of every PIT and creature-selection visual; D-050 and appearance in the working edition do not satisfy this review.
