## ENQ-01 — Draft for the project owner's approved sending account

**To:** info@awems.org.au  
**Subject:** Non-binding reviewer referral enquiry — personal Victorian bush-emergency guide

Hello,

I am developing a personal-use guide for an untrained person who becomes lost, injured or stranded in the Victorian bush. It is not a commercial publication. The research and drafting are AI-assisted; none of the medical content is approved for use.

Could you suggest an Australian-registered clinician with current remote or wilderness emergency experience who might consider a small, independent manuscript review?

The proposed first stage is limited to the initial response, urgent bleeding versus the resuscitation sequence, and the connection between immediate care, communication and necessary movement. The writing must be plain English and distinguish care with a first-aid kit from care without the required supplies.

I can supply a clearly marked review excerpt of no more than 2,500 words, its evidence/conflict notes and up to three proposed diagram outlines. I would first like to establish availability, suitable scope and an indicative fee or capped quotation. Written amendments, exclusions and any referrals beyond the reviewer's expertise would be useful; a later layout check would be a separate quoted item.

This is a non-binding enquiry. No paid work is authorised, and I am not seeking AWEMS endorsement or free clinical review. Please let me know before any chargeable scoping work. If this is outside your referral role, a brief indication is enough.

Thank you.

## ENQ-02 — Draft for the project owner's approved sending account

**To:** convener@bsar.org  
**Subject:** Non-binding referral enquiry — Victorian lost-person guidance

Hello,

I am developing a personal-use Victorian bush-emergency guide for beginners, regardless of the activity that brought them into the bush. The research and drafting are AI-assisted, and the draft is not approved emergency guidance.

Could you advise whether there is an appropriate route to a suitably experienced Victorian land-search practitioner who may be willing to review a small section, in an agreed professional or personal capacity?

The initial questions are when to stop or make a limited move, when and how to request help, how to describe an uncertain location, and how to remain findable after reporting a position. The draft does not propose a general walkout, lone messenger, improvised casualty carry or guaranteed signal range.

If someone is interested, I can supply a marked excerpt of no more than 2,500 words with its source/conflict notes. I would first like to establish the appropriate scope, availability and any review fee. Medical and bushfire decisions outside that person's expertise would be referred separately.

This is a non-binding enquiry, not a request for free volunteer review, operational information about a current search, or endorsement by BSAR or Victoria Police. No paid work is authorised. Please let me know before any chargeable scoping work; if this is not a service you can help with, a brief indication is enough.

Thank you.
