# Specialist review pack

**Prepared:** 4 September 2026  
**Applies to:** Victoria personal edition, emergency-core review prototype  
**Status:** briefs prepared; no reviewer appointed and no content approved

## Purpose

Each brief asks a specialist to answer a defined set of questions. It does not ask for general endorsement of the book. Review the listed content IDs, exact wording, diagrams and how the pages work together, only within your expertise.

Use [the plain-language rules](../../EDITORIAL_STYLE_GUIDE.md) for replacement book wording, including existing drafts. Specialist terms may remain in your evidence notes. Explain terms that the reader needs, and keep all conditions, doses, timing and warnings. If simpler wording loses safety meaning, mark it for revision rather than accepting it because it reads easily.

The controlling files are:

- `CONTENT_VALIDATION_GATE.md`;
- `CONTENT_REGISTER.md`;
- `research/CROSS_PACKET_EMERGENCY_CORE_AUDIT.md`;
- evidence packets `research/evidence-packets/EP-001` to `EP-015`, restricted to the assigned topic;
- `FIRST_AID_KIT_REGISTER.md`;
- `SAFETY_REVIEW.md`;
- the dated review-only prototype supplied with the engagement.

## Minimum reviewer record

For every review, record:

- reviewer name, role, qualifications, current relevant practice and conflicts of interest;
- exact file/version/hash and page/content IDs reviewed;
- sources or guidance checked and their dates;
- each item accepted, amended, rejected or referred outside scope;
- exact replacement wording or marked-up diagram where relevant;
- unresolved reservations and conditions on use;
- review and expiry/recheck date;
- whether post-layout re-review is required (it is mandatory for Level 3 content).

Silence is not approval. General praise, a training certificate or a review of an earlier draft does not approve later wording.

## Required decision labels

- **Accept for post-layout review:** evidence and wording are appropriate within the named scope, subject to layout/usability verification.
- **Amend:** reviewer supplies or approves exact replacement wording/visual change.
- **Reject:** the proposition is unsafe, unsupported or unsuitable for a static lay guide.
- **Refer:** outside the reviewer’s competence; identify the required discipline.
- **Cannot determine:** evidence or scenario is insufficient; content remains excluded.

Only the project register may change a content status, after the signed review outcome and required tests are recorded.
