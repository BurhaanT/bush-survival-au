# RB-004 — Anaphylaxis, allergy and prescribed-device review

**Required reviewers:** Australian allergy/anaphylaxis clinician plus pharmacist or appropriately qualified product/device reviewer.  
**Review object:** dated anaphylaxis module, D-043/D-052/D-062 three-card set (IM-191–IM-193), the IM-205 serious-deterioration card at its anaphylaxis interface, linked asthma/tick/insect pages and every separately proposed product image/instruction.  
**Scope boundary:** this review does not authorise a generic prescription medicine, replace the person’s current action plan or turn any card into product training. IM-191 (recognition/immediate action), IM-192 (ordered positioning) and IM-193 (five-minute/follow-up) deliberately show no brand, dose, route, cap/end, activation, hold time, anatomical target, age/weight rule or ampoule-and-syringe method.

## Content IDs in scope

- `ANAPH-001` to `ANAPH-006`;
- `ASTH-004`;
- `TICK-001` to `TICK-003` and `INSECT-002` at the allergy interface;
- `KIT-010`, `KIT-025`, `KIT-026` and any related image records;
- D-043/D-052/D-062 and `IM-191` to `IM-193`, including each warning, 95% source-match/60% new-layout subjective estimates, accessibility text, caption, cross-card navigation, detached semantic-panel `pageSliceNotes` and Chapter 6 placement. IM-192 specifically maps the previously planned `IM-024` position inset.
- D-069/`IM-205` only at the anaphylaxis interface: absent rash cannot reassure, and the generic conscious-person position cannot override IM-192 or delay adrenaline and 000.

## Decisions requested

1. Confirm lay recognition when skin signs are absent and approve exact cautious wording for severe persistent abdominal pain or repeated vomiting given the difference between simplified insect-sting wording and detailed 2026 any-cause guidance.
2. Approve IM-192’s ordered position branches: CPR gate; pregnancy left side; vomiting or unresponsive-normal-breathing side position; difficult-breathing legs-out; otherwise flat; and the infant/young-child modifiers. Confirm the prohibition on standing, walking, chair sitting and upright/over-shoulder child holding.
3. Confirm IM-191 recognition and IM-193 product-neutral “adrenaline first” wording when a person with known asthma and a known severe allergy develops severe sudden breathing difficulty after possible contact with something they are severely allergic to; approve the IM-192 position transition and make sure an asthma reliever never displaces adrenaline.
4. Confirm adrenaline-before-000 for a solo rescuer, concurrent helper calling, the five-minute “not improved, remains or returns” wording, continued ambulance/hospital care after improvement and at least four hours of observation after the last dose.
5. Confirm that CPR is shown only for **unresponsive and not breathing normally**, despite the February 2026 ASCIA first-aid poster’s “or” wording, and that IM-191–IM-193 link rather than duplicate full CPR mechanics.
6. For every Australian product included at review date—currently identified in ASCIA material as EpiPen, Anapen, Jext and neffy—verify current availability, age/weight/dose scope, route, storage, checking, expiry and exact instructions. Exclude any product not freshly verified; July 2026 availability cannot be presumed current indefinitely.
7. Decide whether the guide should remain wholly product neutral, as IM-191–IM-193 currently do. If product mechanics are later proposed, require separate exact-product panels and never a hybrid injection/nasal drawing.
8. Confirm the no-product branch: no first-aid substitute, immediate 000, correct position, breathing observation and safe no-delay exposure prevention only. Test the failed-000/no-two-way-contact route both with and without an available device, including the instruction not to leave the person merely to seek reception. Decide whether expired, different or another person’s product belongs anywhere in this lay guide.
9. Resolve tick freeze/leave/no-product advice, anatomical exclusions and the conflict between ANZCOR remote-forceps wording and ASCIA/healthdirect advice.
10. Review cold-pack use only as local symptom care and ensure it cannot obscure systemic allergy or disturb an attached tick.
11. Decide whether any freezing aerosol is suitable for a hot Victorian vehicle kit after exact label, ignition and sensitive-site review.
12. Review the IM-205 handover into the anaphylaxis cards. Confirm that no rash is required, its example-sign list is not an all-clear, and its generic lying-or-chair branch stops when the anaphylaxis position applies. Test that a reader does not delay adrenaline, 000 or IM-192 while finishing IM-205.

## Required output

- decision label and exact wording for every in-scope content ID;
- a line-by-line D-043/D-052/D-062 decision for IM-191–IM-193, including branch ownership, cross-card navigation, warning language, detached `pageSliceNotes`, 95%/60% percentages and whether any further split/reordering is required;
- one approved product-neutral three-card recognition/position/overlap system, or an explicit rejection and replacement specification;
- product-by-product version table with source/instruction date and recheck trigger;
- exact inclusions/exclusions for the personal kit;
- corrections to all product and tick-removal visuals;
- mandatory post-layout review, inert-trainer practical tests, stressed-reader tests and pre-edition currentness review.
