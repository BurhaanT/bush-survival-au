## Evidence-confidence labels

The founder requested a percentage estimate. No calibrated probability model exists for “this survival instruction is accurate”, and a made-up number can falsely reassure. This manuscript instead uses High, Moderate or Insufficient, always with the reason and limitation. This deviation was explained; it is not recorded as an explicit founder preference change.

High refers to agreement with the named guidance, not high-quality experimental evidence, expert approval or chance of survival. Moderate identifies a conditional synthesis or untested application. Insufficient prevents a speculative procedure, while preserving supported alternatives.

## Errors or overstatements corrected

- **Bleeding order:** Current ANZCOR 9.1.1 explicitly gives life-threatening bleeding priority over airway/breathing. Earlier notes incorrectly treated the basic ANZCOR/St John relationship as unresolved. The user-facing chapter now states the exception.
- **Compression depth:** Current ANZCOR stresses adequate depth and deliberately does not set a 6 cm maximum. The draft now follows its wording rather than importing an international maximum.
- **Recovery position:** Kept one named adult/child movement sequence rather than citing a different sequence for the original draft's roll-towards variant.
- **Research versus writing:** Removed the rule that no high-consequence prose could be drafted until expert approval. Retained the distinct field-release gate.
- **Water/source links:** Corrected the actual local water-carriage assessment path; source retrieval failures are not called successful checks.
- **No-kit equivalence:** Explicitly state where missing adrenaline, reliever medicine, water treatment or specialist equipment cannot be replaced by ordinary materials.

## 29 September 2026 addendum — carried-sheet shelter and drowning working cards

The current working edition contains 137 visuals through IM-198: 98 external images and 39 original diagrams, used in 138 chapter placements. Chapter 5 contains 4 placements, Chapter 6 contains 18, and the source register contains 483 entries.

D-054 adds IM-196 as a visibly unapproved carried-sheet setup/check card. Its first decision is whether the exact intact equipment, known and practised arrangement, mild-enough screened site and safe personal capacity are all present. If not, the card ends at a no-build fallback. It gives no dimensions, knots, anchor substitutions, wind or temperature rating, no-equipment construction or natural-material shelter method. This closes a navigation gap without presenting an untested geometry as a universal shelter design.
