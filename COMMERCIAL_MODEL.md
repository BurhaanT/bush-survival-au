# Commercial model

> **Archived:** Commercial publication was parked on 4 September 2026. These figures are retained only in case that decision is revisited.

**Version:** Gate 1, 3 September 2026  
**Currency:** AUD unless marked otherwise  
**Status:** Indicative. All scenarios are planning cases, not forecasts.

## Decision summary

A comprehensive premium-colour KDP-first edition is not attractive at incumbent market prices. The model becomes potentially workable only if the product supports a premium price, stays short and earns materially more through short-run/direct sales.

## KDP assumptions

- Regular trim, proposed 6 × 9 inches.
- 192 interior pages.
- Premium colour: A$2.42 fixed + A$0.085/page = **A$18.74**.
- Black and white: A$2.42 fixed + A$0.022/page = **A$6.64**.
- Australian displayed retail price assumed to include 10% GST.
- Royalty tier: 60% of tax-exclusive list price because all tested prices exceed the A$13.99 threshold.
- Standard colour is not currently available through KDP on Amazon.com.au.

Formula used:

`Approx. contribution = 60% × (customer price ÷ 1.10) − print cost`

Sources: [KDP printing costs](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834340), [colour options](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GX56BFPW4BKNPGFW), [paperback royalties](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834330), [GST](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GJX5NGJX75QBTJM9).

## Kindle sensitivity

Assumptions: displayed A$14.99 price includes GST, 70% royalty option and A$0.15/MB delivery. Royalty is modelled as `70% × (tax-exclusive price − delivery)`.

| File size | Delivery | Approx. royalty |
|---:|---:|---:|

| 15 MB | A$2.25 | A$7.96 |
| 30 MB | A$4.50 | A$6.39 |
| 45 MB | A$6.75 | A$4.81 |

At roughly 45 MB the 70% and 35% outcomes converge. Actual file size and visual legibility must be tested. Sources: [KDP eBook royalties](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634500), [70% requirements](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634560).

## Channel strategy to test

| Channel | Role | Economic issue | Gate 2 evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| KDP Amazon AU | Proofs, search discovery, no-inventory long tail | Premium colour and 60% formula create thin margin. | Physical quality and price conversion. |
| Direct website | Best margin, customer relationship and bundles | Inventory, fulfilment and paid acquisition risk. | Fully landed quote, CAC and preorder conversion. |
| Outdoor stores / visitor centres | Trust, discovery near use case | 40–50% discount, low initial orders, possible returns. | Three quantity-bearing expressions of interest. |
| Clubs / trainers / corporate safety | Bulk sales and credibility | Longer relationship sales cycle; content scrutiny. | Two partner conversations and indicative order size. |
| Ingram / general bookstores | Broad availability | Wholesale discount plus print; return exposure. | Live compensation calculation; avoid returnable status initially unless justified. |
| Kindle/ePub/PDF | Low manufacturing cost and instant updates | Image delivery, navigation, piracy and field device dependence. | Working 15/30 MB prototype on phone and Kindle. |

## Financial gate

Before full production, require:

- validated retail price of at least A$39.95;
- contribution target of at least A$12 for an Amazon/retail-compatible sale or A$18 direct;
- a full cash budget based on quotes;
- a base-case three-year break-even without future-series revenue;
- a maximum founder-time budget;
- an explicit inventory-loss downside case;
- CAC evidence from the validation campaign rather than an assumed organic launch.
