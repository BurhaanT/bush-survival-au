# Non-medical equipment capability register

**Status:** Working register — no product is approved merely by appearing here  
**Scope:** Personal Victorian guide; activity-agnostic emergency capability  
**Medical supplies:** controlled separately in `FIRST_AID_KIT_REGISTER.md`

## Purpose

This register prevents the book from treating a generic “survival kit” as a magic object. Each item must perform a named task under known Victorian conditions, work with the other items, and retain a defensible fallback when it is lost, flat, wet, frozen, overheated, damaged, clogged, expired or inaccessible.

The eventual guide may show three carriage layers:

1. **On person:** capabilities that remain with the reader after separation from a pack, vehicle, camp or boat.
2. **Primary carried/vehicle kit:** the normal emergency system.
3. **Redundant or group capability:** a second method that does not fail for the same reason.

The layers are activity-agnostic. Their weight, capacity and environmental rating may change with remoteness, season, terrain, group needs and forecast—not with a chapter labelled hiking, 4WD or fishing.

## Required product record

Before a particular model enters reader-facing guidance, record:

- exact manufacturer, model, revision, size/capacity and serial/batch where relevant;
- precise task and related content IDs;
- authoritative manual and independent performance/certification evidence;
- conditions and hazards it does not cover;
- training/practice requirement;
- dependent consumables, subscriptions, network, batteries, fuel, accessories and companion devices;
- operating/storage temperature, water/dust/impact limits and shelf/service life;
- inspection, maintenance, recharge, registration, firmware, expiry and replacement schedule;
- failure indication and safe fallback;
- one-person and group field-test record, including gloves, darkness, wet, heat, cold and fatigue as relevant;
- diagram/photo asset, rights and factual review status;
- post-layout check and currentness trigger.

Marketing language such as “survival grade”, “all weather”, “purifies”, “satellite enabled” or “military” is not performance evidence.

## System interactions that must be tested

- The phone cable must actually charge every intended device; “USB” alone is not compatibility.
- Satellite communication and PLB must not share a single inaccessible storage location or be described as equivalent.
- A satellite device, active-looking account and paired app are not a working system until the exact provider-sanctioned satellite test succeeds; a queued/sent/acknowledged state is not responder arrival.
- Shelter, insulation, clothing and water treatment must remain reachable without unpacking the entire kit in rain, wind or darkness.
- The water vessel must be stable on the exact appliance; the appliance can only be used outdoors under current fire rules.
- Potable water must remain with the person in at least two independently closed vessels; a vehicle or camp cache never substitutes for the separated-person reserve.
- Flexible clean-water capacity must not be the sole container and must stay within its temperature/cleaning instructions; Watercell X never receives boiling water.
- Dirty water equipment must not be stored where leakage contaminates first-aid supplies, food or clean vessels.
- Dirty/clean roles must differ by words, touch, shape and storage rather than colour or memory alone; emptied clean containers do not switch to dirty use.
- A treatment sequence that depends on a membrane plus tablets needs an exact independently reconciled formulation, volume, timer and separate clean vessel; a second strip of the same tablets is consumable redundancy, not an independent backup.
- A filter integrity check is valid only after the exact preparation/wet-out conditions; clogged/no-flow, trapped-air bubbles and membrane damage must not be merged.
- Boiling counts as water-treatment backup only when the exact outdoor heat system is lawful, fuelled and stable; the unresolved TFB meal-purpose exception cannot be transferred to drinking-water treatment.
- Fire-control water required for stove use is budgeted separately and cannot be double-counted as potable reserve; ten litres available never guarantees extinction.
- Do not add a windscreen or reflector that the exact stove manual prohibits; poor wind performance makes that heat path unavailable.
- No treatment product or carbon component overrides the flood, algal, chemical, mining/agricultural/industrial, fire-impact or salt source-rejection branch.
- Batteries, aerosols, medicines, adhesives, filters and stored water must be assessed for hot-vehicle exposure.
- A navigation or lighting capability must never silently change the upstream stay/move decision.
- Equipment used for signalling must not require leaving shelter or entering exposed terrain without the hazard decision being revisited.
- A group bothy must not be counted as the personal cold enclosure or ground insulation for any occupant.
- A cold-weather bivvy/vapour barrier must not be repurposed as hot-weather shade without an independently reviewed ventilation method.
- A roll-top dry bag protects only when the exact closure is correctly made and undamaged; “waterproof fabric” is not a submersion promise.

## No-equipment rule

The book may describe a no-equipment or lost-equipment path only after the exact improvised action passes the same gate. “Use what you have” is not sufficient. If a safe function cannot be improvised—for example satellite distress alert, verified chemical removal or a dependable treatment device—the guide must say the capability is absent and explain the consequence for rescue, movement or risk.

## Selection sequence

1. Complete the relevant evidence packet and define the capability, failure modes and exclusions.
2. Shortlist products only from evidence matching that capability.
3. Record manuals, certification, current price/availability and environmental/storage limits.
4. Bench-test and field-test usability without exposing people to the hazard the item claims to control.
5. Obtain the required technical/operational review.
6. Approve the product record, exact words and visual together.
7. Recheck after layout and on every listed trigger.
